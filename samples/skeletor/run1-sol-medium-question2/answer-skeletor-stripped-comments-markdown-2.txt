When multiple files contain the anchor text, relocation chooses **one** candidate. It ranks candidates by matched text coverage, then by matching shingle hits, then by lowest document ID. It gives the original file no preference and does not flag a tie as ambiguous. The chosen match becomes the anchor’s new location ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:348), [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:483)).

That is deterministic, but it is **not desirable when identical text appears in unrelated files**: the anchor can silently move to the wrong copy. A test documents this as a known false positive ([Text_api_tests.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/tests/Text_api_tests.ml:1096)). A safer policy would use the original location and surrounding context to distinguish matches, and leave the anchor for review when the destination remains ambiguous.
