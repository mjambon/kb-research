If the anchor text matches in multiple files, relocation picks **one** candidate. It ranks candidates by shingle coverage, then hit count, then document ID; it does not prefer the anchor’s original file or treat multiple exact matches as ambiguous. The chosen candidate becomes the anchor’s new location and is marked up to date. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-sitemap/src/skeletor/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:348) [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-sitemap/src/skeletor/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:483)

That is **not desirable when the copies have different meanings**: an anchor can silently move to an unrelated file. The tests document this as a known false positive. A safer policy would keep an exact match in the original file when available and flag unresolved cross-file ties for review.
