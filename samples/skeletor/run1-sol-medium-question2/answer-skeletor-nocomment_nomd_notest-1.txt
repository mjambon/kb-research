If the anchor text matches in multiple files, relocation **automatically picks one**. It ranks candidates by matched text coverage, then by the number of matching hits, and finally by the lowest document ID. It gives the original file no special preference. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-nocomment_nomd_notest/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:344)

That behavior is risky when the matches are equally plausible: the chosen file may contain an unrelated copy of the text. A test documents exactly that false positive. Cross-file relocation is useful when the destination is clear, but an ambiguous match would be better left for review than assigned by a document-ID tie break. [Text_api_tests.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-nocomment_nomd_notest/base-libs/text/tests/Text_api_tests.ml:1097)
