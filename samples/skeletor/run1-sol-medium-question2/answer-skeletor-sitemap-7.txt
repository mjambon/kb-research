When relocation finds matches in multiple files, it ranks the candidate files by matched-text coverage, then by the number of supporting hits, then by lowest document ID. It relocates the anchor to the first candidate. There is **no preference for the original file** and no ambiguity warning; a successful relocation is marked up to date and does not need review. See [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-sitemap/src/skeletor/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:348) and [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-sitemap/src/skeletor/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:483).

That is risky for a persistent reference: identical text does not establish that two occurrences are the same piece of code. The tests document a known false positive where an anchor moves to an unrelated file containing a verbatim copy. A safer policy would keep an exact match in the original file when available and flag genuinely ambiguous moves for review.
