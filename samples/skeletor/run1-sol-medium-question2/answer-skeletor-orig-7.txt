Anchor relocation treats matches in multiple files as competing candidates. It chooses the one with the greatest matched text coverage, then the most matching hits, then the lowest document ID. It gives the original file no preference and does not report ambiguity. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:348)

That is deterministic, but it can be wrong: identical text does not establish which copy the anchor refers to. The tests document a case where an anchor relocates to unrelated boilerplate in another file. [Text_api_tests.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/tests/Text_api_tests.ml:1096) For equally plausible destinations, leaving the anchor outdated for review would be safer than silently choosing one.
