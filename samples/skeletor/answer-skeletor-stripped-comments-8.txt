When multiple files contain the anchor text, relocation chooses **one** candidate. It ranks candidates by matched text coverage, then by the number of matching hits, and finally by lowest document ID. It gives the original file no special preference, and updates the anchor to the winner. That is deterministic, but **not desirable when the matches are genuinely ambiguous**: the chosen copy may be unrelated to the original evidence. A test records this as a known false positive. A safer policy would keep a valid match in the original file and flag unresolved cross-file ties for review.
