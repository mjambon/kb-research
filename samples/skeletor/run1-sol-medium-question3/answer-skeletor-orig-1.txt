I found three anchor relocation issues:

- **P0 — An unrelated copy can become current evidence.** Relocation searches every indexed document and chooses by text match, with no source or origin check ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:355)). If an anchor’s text is removed while the same boilerplate already exists in another file, the anchor moves there and becomes `Up_to_date`. A test confirms this false positive ([Text_api_tests.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/tests/Text_api_tests.ml:1098)).

- **P0 — Relocation can suppress future stale-anchor review.** Moving an anchor to a document with a lower revision preserves its `acknowledged_revision` ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:505), [DB.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/DB.ml:805)). If relocation later fails, `needs_review` compares that old acknowledgement against the new document’s revision ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:109)). The stale anchor can remain absent from the review list until the destination reaches the old revision number.

- **P1 — A destination indexed later does not trigger a retry.** Failed relocation records an attempt against the original document’s revision; retries run only when that document advances ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:490), [DB.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/DB.ml:851)). Deleting the old document and then indexing its replacement leaves the anchor stale, potentially indefinitely, unless the old document changes again.
