- **P0 — An anchor can silently move to unrelated text.** Relocation searches shingles across all indexed documents and selects the highest coverage candidate without checking source or preferring the original document ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:199), [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:348)). A test demonstrates an anchor moving to an unrelated file that shares a verbatim block after its original text is replaced ([Text_api_tests.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/tests/Text_api_tests.ml:1096)). The move marks the anchor current and replaces its stored contents, so the incorrect citation no longer appears outdated.

- **P1 — A failed relocation is not retried when a destination arrives later.** Relocation runs only for anchors attached to the document being updated ([Text.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Text.ml:199), [DB.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/DB.ml:852)). If the original document is updated before the moved text is indexed elsewhere, the attempt fails; indexing the destination later does not revisit that anchor. It remains outdated until the original document’s revision changes again.
