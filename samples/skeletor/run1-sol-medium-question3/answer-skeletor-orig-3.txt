I found three issues worth fixing:

- **P0 — An unchanged anchor can jump to a duplicate in another document.** Candidate ranking breaks equal matches by document ID and gives the anchor’s current document no preference. If document A was indexed before B and both contain the same anchored text, an edit elsewhere in B can move B’s anchor to A even though its text in B is unchanged. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:348)

- **P0 — A move can be missed permanently when the destination is indexed later.** A failed relocation records the attempted revision. Relocation then runs only for anchors on the document being updated. If the old document is deleted before the new document is indexed, adding the destination does not retry the stale anchor; a deleted old document may never trigger another attempt. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:490), [DB.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/DB.ml:852)

- **P1 — Relocation can suppress later review of a stale anchor.** Relocation preserves `acknowledged_revision` when moving an anchor to another document. That revision belongs to the old document. If it is higher than the destination’s revision, a later failed relocation on the destination leaves `needs_review` false until its revision catches up. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:505), [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:109)
