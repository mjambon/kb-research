I found four potential anchor relocation issues:

- **P0 — An unrelated edit can move an intact anchor to another file.** Candidates are ranked by text coverage and document ID, with no preference for the current document. If an earlier indexed file contains the same text, editing bytes outside the anchor can move it there. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-cm+answer/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:355)
- **P0 — Relocation can cross registered source boundaries.** The shingle lookup searches all documents without filtering by the anchor’s source. Deleting text in one source can attach its anchor to an identical snippet in an unrelated source. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-cm+answer/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:206)
- **P0 — Relocation can suppress review of a later stale anchor.** Relocation preserves `acknowledged_revision` when changing documents. An acknowledgment from a high revision document can therefore hide a failed relocation on a lower revision destination until its revision passes that old number. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-cm+answer/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:512)
- **P1 — Repeated text can become outdated after an unrelated edit.** Indexing drops every occurrence of a repeated shingle. A short anchor appearing twice may have no searchable hits even while both copies remain intact. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-cm+answer/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:159)
