- **P0 — An unrelated copy can take over an intact anchor.** Candidate ranking breaks ties by document ID, with no preference for the anchor’s current document. If an earlier indexed document contains the same text, an edit elsewhere in the anchor’s document can move the anchor to that unrelated copy even though its original text is still present. See [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:348) and [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:495).

- **P1 — An anchor can remain stale after its text appears in a newly indexed document.** Relocation runs only for anchors assigned to the document being updated. If an anchor’s original document was deleted and a different document containing its text is indexed later, that new document’s update never retries the anchor. See [Text.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Text.ml:212) and [DB.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/DB.ml:852).

- **P1 — Moving an acknowledged anchor can suppress later review.** Relocation retains `acknowledged_revision`, but revisions belong to individual documents. After a move from a high revision document to a low revision one, a subsequent failed relocation can remain hidden from `needs_review` until the destination’s revision exceeds the old acknowledgement number. See [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:109) and [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:505).
