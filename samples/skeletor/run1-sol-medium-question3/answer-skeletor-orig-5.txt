- **P0 — An anchor can move away from text that is still present.** When an unrelated part of its document changes, relocation searches every indexed document. If another document contains the same anchored text, both can be equally strong candidates, and the lower document ID wins. The anchor then points to the copy even though its original text remains intact. See [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:348).

- **P1 — Short valid anchors can become stale after an unrelated edit.** Anchors as short as 16 normalized bytes are accepted, but document shingles are indexed every eight bytes. A 16-byte span starting between index positions has no matching indexed shingle, even when its text is unchanged. See [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:152) and [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:554).

- **P1 — A destination indexed later does not revive a stale anchor.** A failed relocation advances the attempt revision. Adding the matching destination afterward only checks anchors already assigned to that destination; the stale anchor on the original document is not retried until that original document changes again. See [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:490) and [Text.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Text.ml:211).
