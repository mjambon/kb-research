### Potential issues

- **P0 — Acknowledged anchors can become outdated without needing review after a move.** Relocation changes `doc_id` but preserves `acknowledged_revision` ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:505)). Review status then compares that revision with the *new* document’s revision ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:109)). If an anchor acknowledged at revision 10 moves to a document at revision 0, a failed relocation after that document reaches revision 1 is marked outdated but does not need review.

- **P0 — Relocation can silently attach evidence to unrelated text.** The matcher accepts a sufficiently strong text match in another document and applies the first candidate ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:483)). An existing test demonstrates an anchor moving to an unrelated copy of shared boilerplate after its original text is replaced ([Text_api_tests.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/tests/Text_api_tests.ml:1096)). The resulting anchor appears up to date despite pointing at unrelated evidence.

- **P1 — Relocation depends on document update order.** Only anchors belonging to the document currently being indexed are retried ([Text.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Text.ml:199), [DB.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/DB.ml:852)). If the original document changes before a new destination is indexed, its anchor fails relocation; adding the destination later does not retry it. The anchor remains outdated until the original document’s revision changes again.
