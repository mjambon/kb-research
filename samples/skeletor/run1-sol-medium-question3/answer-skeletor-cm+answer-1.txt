I found four potential issues in anchor relocation:

- **P0 — An intact anchor can move to a duplicate in another file.** Candidates are ranked by match coverage, hits, then document ID, with no preference for the current document. Editing bytes outside the anchored span can therefore move it to an earlier indexed copy. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-cm+answer/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:355)

- **P0 — Relocation can cross registered source boundaries.** The global shingle search does not filter by the anchor’s source. Removing a snippet from one repository can attach its anchor to identical, unrelated text in another. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-cm+answer/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:206)

- **P0 — A later failed relocation can be hidden from review.** Relocation retains `acknowledged_revision` when changing documents. If the old document’s revision is higher than the destination’s, a subsequent failed relocation can remain `needs_review = false` until the destination passes that old revision number. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-cm+answer/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:512) [DB.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-cm+answer/base-libs/text/DB.ml:805)

- **P1 — Repeated text can become stale after an unrelated edit.** Indexing drops every occurrence of a repeated shingle. A short anchor appearing twice can consequently have no candidate hits even though both copies remain intact. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-cm+answer/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:159)
