I found three potential issues:

- **P0 — A short anchor can move to the wrong document after an unrelated edit.** Document shingles are indexed every 8 bytes, while a 16-byte anchor contributes just one query shingle. If that anchor starts at byte 1, its shingle is absent from its own document’s index. When the document changes elsewhere, relocation can select an indexed copy in another document even though the original text is still present. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-sitemap/src/skeletor/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:167), [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-sitemap/src/skeletor/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:355)

- **P0 — Relocation can suppress a later review request.** Moving an anchor to another document preserves `acknowledged_revision`, but document revision numbers are local to each document. If the old document was acknowledged at revision 10 and the destination is at revision 0, a failed relocation at destination revision 1 is marked outdated yet `needs_review` remains false. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-sitemap/src/skeletor/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:109), [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-sitemap/src/skeletor/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:505)

- **P1 — A failed move is not retried when the destination is indexed later.** Relocation checks anchors belonging to the document being updated. If the original is removed before the new document is indexed, the failed attempt advances its attempt revision; indexing the destination does not revisit that anchor. It remains outdated until the original document changes again. [Text.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-sitemap/src/skeletor/base-libs/text/Text.ml:199), [DB.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-sitemap/src/skeletor/base-libs/text/DB.ml:852)
