I found four potential issues in anchor relocation:

- **P0 — An unrelated edit can move an intact anchor to another file.** Relocation searches all documents, then breaks equally ranked matches by document ID. If a duplicate was indexed first, editing bytes outside the anchored span can move the anchor to that duplicate. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-cm+answer/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:355)

- **P0 — An anchor can attach to text from another source.** Candidate collection uses the global shingle index without checking the anchor’s source. Deleting a snippet in one registered source can attach its anchor to identical, unrelated text in another. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-cm+answer/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:206)

- **P0 — Relocation can suppress a later review warning.** Relocation changes the document but preserves `acknowledged_revision`. After a move from a high revision document to a lower revision one, a failed relocation can leave an outdated anchor with `needs_review = false`. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-cm+answer/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:512)

- **P1 — Repeated text can make an intact anchor stale.** Indexing drops every occurrence of a repeated shingle. A short anchor with repeated text may therefore get no candidate hits after an unrelated edit, even though its text remains present. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-cm+answer/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:155)
