Relocation searches indexed documents and picks **one** candidate. It ranks candidates by matched text coverage, then matching hits, then lowest document ID; it does not flag multiple exact matches as ambiguous or prefer the anchor’s original file. Because indexing samples shingles, even exact copies can receive different scores. The chosen anchor is then treated as up to date, with no review flag. I don’t think that is desirable for evidence links: the winning copy may be unrelated to the original. A test documents that false positive. A safer policy would keep an exact match in the original file when possible and surface unresolved cross-file ambiguity for review.
