- **P0 — An unchanged anchor can move to another document.** Candidate ranking has no preference for the anchor’s current document; equal matches fall back to the lowest document ID. If two documents contain the same passage and the document holding the anchor is reindexed, relocation can move the anchor to the older document even though its passage is still present. See [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:348).

- **P1 — Short anchors can fail to relocate after a simple insertion.** Document shingles are indexed every eight bytes, while a 16-byte anchor has only one query shingle. If that exact passage begins between indexed positions, relocation finds no hit and marks the anchor outdated. For example, inserting one byte before a 16-byte passage can cause this. See [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:167).
