I found four potential issues:

- **P0 — A move can be missed because relocation depends on update order.** Each file is reindexed and its anchors relocated immediately ([Text.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Text.ml:199)). If text moves from A to B and A is processed first, relocation fails before B is indexed. The failed attempt records A’s current revision ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:491)); indexing B later does not retry it. A no-op update of A will not retry either.

- **P0 — Moving an anchor retains an acknowledgment from the old document.** Relocation changes `doc_id` but preserves `acknowledged_revision` ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:505)). If the old document was acknowledged at revision 5 and the destination is at revision 0, a later failed relocation at destination revision 1 is marked as needing no review, because that check compares revision 5 with revision 1 ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:109)).

- **P1 — A valid 16-byte anchor may have no searchable shingle.** Document shingles are indexed only every eight bytes ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:167)). A 16-byte anchor beginning at byte 1 has just one query shingle, which is absent from that index; it can become outdated after an unrelated edit even while its text remains intact.

- **P1 — Repeated text can make an intact anchor unrelocatable.** Indexing discards *every* occurrence of a shingle that repeats within a document ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:163)). An anchor on a repeated block can therefore have no hits, even when its original block is still present.
