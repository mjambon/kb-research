- **P0 — Relocation depends on update order.** [Documents_update_cmd.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-sitemap/src/skeletor/cli/Documents_update_cmd.ml:438) indexes files one at a time, and [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-sitemap/src/skeletor/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:525) retries an anchor only when its current document changes. If text moves from `a.ml` to a newly added `z.ml` in one update, `a.ml` can be processed before `z.ml` exists in the index. The anchor becomes outdated and later updates of `z.ml` do not retry it.

- **P0 — An unrelated copy can silently become the destination.** [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-sitemap/src/skeletor/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:483) searches the whole corpus and immediately stores the top text match as current. Replacing an anchor’s original text can therefore attach it to a preexisting, unrelated copy in another file. [A test documents this false positive](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-sitemap/src/skeletor/base-libs/text/tests/Text_api_tests.ml:1098); the resulting anchor is marked up to date rather than needing review.

- **P1 — Repeated text cannot relocate even when an exact copy exists.** [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-sitemap/src/skeletor/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:150) discards every occurrence of a repeated query shingle. A repetitive anchor can consequently produce zero hits and remain outdated after an exact move. [The regression test confirms this behavior](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-sitemap/src/skeletor/base-libs/text/tests/Anchor_algorithm_tests.ml:102).
