### Findings

- **P0 — Relocation can silently attach evidence to an unrelated file.** Candidates are ranked by text coverage without preferring the original document or checking whether a cross-file move is plausible. If the original text is replaced and another file contains the same block, the anchor becomes current in that unrelated file. An existing test confirms this case as a known limitation. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:348), [test](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/tests/Text_api_tests.ml:1097)

- **P1 — Cross-file moves depend on indexing order.** Relocation runs only for anchors attached to the document being updated. If the old file is updated before the destination is indexed, the attempt fails; indexing the destination later does not retry that anchor. It stays outdated until the old file’s revision changes again. The move test indexes the destination first. [Text.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Text.ml:199), [DB.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/DB.ml:852)

- **P1 — Acknowledgement can suppress review after a cross-file relocation.** Relocation retains `acknowledged_revision`, although that revision belongs to the old document. If the anchor moves from an acknowledged old document at revision 10 to a new document at revision 0, then fails relocation after the new document reaches revision 1, `needs_review` remains false because it compares 10 with 1. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:109), [DB.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/DB.ml:805)
