I found three potential issues in anchor relocation:

- **P0 — An unchanged duplicate can steal an anchor.** Candidates are ranked by matched coverage, hit count, then document ID, with no preference for the anchor’s current document. If an older document contains the same text, a simple insertion in the anchored document can move the anchor to that older copy even though the original text is still present. The anchor is then marked up to date at the wrong location. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:348)

- **P1 — A destination indexed later does not trigger another attempt.** Relocation runs only for anchors attached to the document being updated. If the original is edited or deleted before the destination is indexed, the failed anchor stays outdated when the destination arrives; it is retried only after another update to the original document. [Text.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Text.ml:211), [DB.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/DB.ml:852)

- **P1 — An old acknowledgement can suppress review after a cross-document move.** Relocation retains `acknowledged_revision` when changing `doc_id`, although revision numbers belong to individual documents. If an anchor acknowledged at revision 100 moves to a document at revision 0, then becomes outdated at revision 1, `needs_review` remains false until that document passes revision 100. [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:109), [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-orig/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:505)
