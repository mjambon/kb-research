I found three potential issues with anchor relocation:

- **P0 — An unrelated copy can become the anchor’s evidence.** Relocation selects the highest coverage candidate across all documents without preferring the original document or checking provenance ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:348)). A [test documents the false positive](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/tests/Text_api_tests.ml:1096): replacing the anchored text in `a.ml` moves its anchor to unrelated boilerplate in `b.ml` and marks it current.

- **P1 — Relocation depends on document indexing order.** A failed attempt is recorded against the original document’s revision ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:490)). Later indexing a matching destination does not retry that anchor; retries only select anchors belonging to the document being updated ([Text.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Text.ml:199), [DB.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/DB.ml:852)). The same move can therefore succeed or remain outdated depending on which file is indexed first.

- **P1 — Repetitive text cannot be relocated, even after a nearby edit.** Query shingles occurring more than once are all discarded ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:152)). An anchor such as 80 repeated `A` bytes produces no hits, so changing only surrounding text leaves it outdated despite its anchored text remaining intact. The [algorithm test](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/tests/Anchor_algorithm_tests.ml:102) confirms this behavior.
