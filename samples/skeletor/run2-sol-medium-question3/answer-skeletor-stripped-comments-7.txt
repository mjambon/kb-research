I found three potential issues:

- **P0 — An anchor can move to the wrong document while its original text is still present.** Candidates are ranked by coverage, hit count, then document ID, with no preference for the anchor’s current document ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:348)). If an older document contains an identical copy, editing the anchored document can silently move the anchor to that copy.

- **P0 — Relocation can permanently miss a document move when the destination is indexed later.** A failed attempt records the source document’s current revision ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:490)). Later indexing the destination only retries anchors assigned to that destination ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:525)). The orphaned anchor therefore stays on the source until that source changes again. This can occur when documents are indexed in an order that processes the source first.

- **P1 — Short anchors often fail after a one byte insertion.** Document shingles are sampled only at offsets divisible by eight, while a 16 byte anchor has just one query shingle ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:152), [Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:199)). Inserting one byte before an otherwise unchanged 16 byte anchor shifts that shingle off the sampling grid, leaving no candidate and marking the anchor outdated.
