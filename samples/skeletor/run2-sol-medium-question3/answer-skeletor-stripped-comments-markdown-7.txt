I found three potential issues:

- **P0 — A relocated anchor can hide a later failed relocation from review.** Relocation preserves `acknowledged_revision` even when moving the anchor to another document ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:505), [DB.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/DB.ml:805)). If the old document was acknowledged at revision 100 and the new document is at revision 0, a failed relocation at revision 1 remains `Outdated` but `needs_review` is false until the new document passes revision 100 ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:109)).

- **P0 — Shared text can silently move an anchor to unrelated evidence.** Candidate selection uses shingle coverage and hit count, with no preference for the original document or check that the text actually moved ([Anchor.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:348)). An existing test demonstrates an anchor relocating to another file’s unrelated copy after its own text is replaced ([Text_api_tests.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/tests/Text_api_tests.ml:1096)).

- **P1 — Moving text between files depends on indexing order.** Each file triggers relocation immediately after its own index update ([Text.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Text.ml:199)), while the update command indexes files sequentially ([Documents_update_cmd.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/cli/Documents_update_cmd.ml:434)). If the source is processed before the destination, relocation fails. Adding the destination does not retry that anchor, and an unchanged source does not advance its revision to trigger another attempt ([DB.ml](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/DB.ml:852)).
