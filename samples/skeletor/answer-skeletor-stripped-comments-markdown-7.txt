Anchor relocation considers matches across indexed files. It chooses the candidate with the most matched-text coverage, then the most matching hits; a tie goes to the **lowest document ID**. It does not prefer the original file or flag the choice as ambiguous. The chosen location is saved and reported as up to date. [Candidate ranking](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:348) · [Relocation](/workspace/kb-research/samples/skeletor/skeletor-stripped-comments-markdown/base-libs/text/Anchor.ml:483)

That is deterministic, but I would not consider it desirable when multiple files contain equally good exact matches: document ID says nothing about which copy the anchor meant. A safer policy would keep a valid match in the original file; if the original no longer matches and several destinations are equally plausible, leave the anchor for review.
